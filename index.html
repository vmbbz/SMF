HOW PLAYERS EARN — AND WHAT COUNTS

1. VERIFY & CHOOSE

Connect a Solana wallet, sign the free StickLash sign-in message, then choose Skill Championship or Boosted League and an input division.

2. FIGHT A RANDOM HUMAN

Public matchmaking pairs online players. The server locks identities and rules, runs the match, freezes the result, and updates only that league and input division.

3. PLACE IN A FUTURE EPOCH

There is no token payment per win. After rewards launch, validated matches form an epoch-only ranking; qualified placements can receive both funded assets after review.

HOW DUAL REWARDS WORK

GAME-TOKEN RESERVE

Funded by 100% of verified game-token boost receipts and clearly labelled sponsor deposits.

$ANSEM RESERVE

Funded by verified creator-fee market buys, Director-action spends, and clearly labelled sponsor deposits.

ONE PLACEMENT, TWO ASSETS

Eligible winners can receive both tokens. Each payout is capped by its own confirmed reserve and pre-published token-unit budget.

The leaderboard decides placement; it does not create funds. Rewards are enabled only after both reserves, server-authoritative results, anti-abuse review, and a verified claim process exist.
